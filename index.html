From handwritten reflections to leadership in action.Leadership begins with understanding yourself. The Playbook is a space for reflection, self-leadership, emotional intelligence, and the everyday experiences that shape how to lead.

Why do small moments shake us so much?...Why did that bother me?

A criticism can stay with us all day. Being left out can feel personal. A small disruption can trigger a strong reaction. Often, what bothers us isn't really about the moment. It's about what the moment triggers inside us—insecurities, fears, expectations, identity, ego, and our need for control. INSPIRED BY SIMON SINEK
